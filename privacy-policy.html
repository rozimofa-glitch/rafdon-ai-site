Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

1. Information we process

RAFDON AI may process information you provide directly and information made available by TikTok after you explicitly authorize the integration, including basic profile information, account statistics, public video information, and public video metrics permitted by the scopes you grant.

2. How we use information

We use authorized information to authenticate your account, synchronize authorized TikTok data into the RAFDON AI analytics workspace, analyze content performance, and support content planning.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Authorized data may be processed by service providers required to operate RAFDON AI, such as hosting and database infrastructure.

4. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. Credentials and API secrets are intended to be stored in secure server-side configuration rather than public source files.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as needed to provide the service and its analytics functions. You may revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok. Requests concerning deletion of RAFDON AI-held data may be submitted to the service operator through the contact information provided with the service.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
